# Vellox Studio Six-Month Social Media Launch Design

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Status:** Approved design, awaiting implementation planning

## 1. Objective

Build a six-month, English-first organic social media system that helps Vellox Studio attract qualified small and medium-sized business clients across the MENA region for three core offers:

- Branding and brand identity
- Social media design
- Website design and development

The campaign must establish trust, demonstrate real capability, and turn attention into direct messages and WhatsApp conversations. It must also give designers complete, unambiguous briefs and provide a simple production workflow that shows the status of every post.

The campaign will not use video during the first six months. Every concept will be a static design, carousel, before-and-after presentation, case-study layout, testimonial design, or other still-image format.

## 2. Audience and Positioning

### Primary audience

Owners and decision-makers at SMBs across the MENA region. The campaign will be sector-inclusive rather than limited to wellness, fintech, or food and beverage. Examples may come from different sectors, but the business problems must remain widely recognizable.

### Audience situations

- Launching a new business or offer
- Operating with an inconsistent or outdated brand
- Posting regularly without a coherent social media system
- Using a website that does not explain the offer or generate enough enquiries
- Growing beyond a do-it-yourself visual identity
- Preparing to reposition, rebrand, or expand

### Vellox position

Vellox is a business development studio that connects strategy, design, and digital execution. The campaign will present Vellox as an accountable, commercially minded partner rather than a supplier of isolated design assets.

### Core campaign promise

Vellox helps MENA businesses become clearer, more credible, and easier to choose through connected branding, social media design, and websites.

## 3. Voice and Visual Direction

### Voice

The tone is clean, confident, concise, and professional. It should feel considered and commercially aware, but not overly friendly or informal.

Use:

- Direct statements
- Specific business problems and outcomes
- Plain English
- Short paragraphs
- Calm confidence
- Useful frameworks and clear recommendations

Avoid:

- Slang and forced friendliness
- Hype, exaggerated promises, and unsupported claims
- Excessive emojis or exclamation marks
- Vague motivational content
- Generic statements that could belong to any agency
- Aggressive selling

### Visual direction

- Strong editorial typography
- Structured grids and generous negative space
- One principal idea per design
- Minimal decorative elements
- Clear hierarchy that survives platform resizing
- Consistent Vellox brand system across all six months
- Portfolio imagery used as evidence, not decoration

Designs must begin from a master layout and then be adapted for LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and X. Platform resizing does not create a separate strategic concept.

## 4. Publishing Model

### Platforms

- LinkedIn
- Instagram
- Facebook
- X

### Production capacity

Six master concepts per week. Each concept is adapted to all four platforms. The core plan contains 24 production weeks, creating 144 complete concepts. Extra calendar days in the six-month period are reserved for performance-led refreshes, seasonal adjustments, or production buffer rather than filler content.

### Weekly rhythm

1. **Problem diagnosis:** identify a familiar brand, social, or website problem.
2. **Educational insight:** explain a principle that helps an SMB make a better decision.
3. **Practical framework:** give a checklist, audit, comparison, or process.
4. **Proof:** show a real project, result, testimonial, or before-and-after example.
5. **Vellox standard:** demonstrate the studio's process, quality criteria, or point of view.
6. **Offer:** invite a relevant DM or WhatsApp conversation.

The sequence repeats weekly, but the service focus and examples change to prevent repetition.

### Platform adaptation

The core idea remains constant, while the caption is rewritten for the platform:

- **LinkedIn:** professional explanation, business context, and decision-making value.
- **Instagram:** concise caption supporting a strong static design or carousel.
- **Facebook:** accessible explanation with practical context for business owners.
- **X:** one sharp idea, a brief supporting statement, and a direct CTA within platform limits.

Captions must not be copied verbatim across all four platforms.

## 5. Six-Month Content Journey

### Month 1 — Recognition

Help business owners identify problems they may have normalized.

- Brand clarity and positioning gaps
- Inconsistent identity and credibility
- Weak or confusing website messaging
- Social media inconsistency
- Signs that a business has outgrown do-it-yourself design
- Introductory Vellox diagnostic offers

### Month 2 — Education

Give practical guidance that demonstrates expertise without overwhelming the reader.

- Brand positioning and message hierarchy
- Identity-system fundamentals
- Social media design systems
- Website structure and conversion basics
- Checklists for launches, rebrands, and redesigns
- How to brief and evaluate creative work

### Month 3 — Proof

Replace general claims with evidence.

- Real branding projects
- Website case studies
- Social media design examples
- Before-and-after comparisons
- Client testimonials
- Results and project lessons that the client has approved for public use

No statistic, quotation, client name, or result may be invented. If proof is unavailable, the post must use an anonymized process lesson without a fabricated outcome.

### Month 4 — Authority

Show how Vellox thinks and works.

- Studio principles and quality standards
- Strategic opinions on common MENA SMB challenges
- The relationship between brand, social content, and the website
- Vellox's discovery, design, review, and launch processes
- Common mistakes in agency and freelancer engagements
- Long-term design-system thinking

### Month 5 — Consideration

Help prospects understand what they need and what working with Vellox involves.

- Service comparisons and selection guidance
- Deliverables and realistic timelines
- What affects project scope
- How revisions and approvals work
- Common objections and decision criteria
- The business value of consistency and clarity

Pricing will not be invented or published unless Vellox supplies approved figures.

### Month 6 — Conversion

Turn established credibility into qualified conversations.

- Brand, website, and social media diagnostic invitations
- Portfolio recaps
- High-performing proof refreshed with stronger CTAs
- FAQ and objection handling
- Clear explanations of engagement fit
- Direct invitations to DM or contact Vellox on WhatsApp

Conversion content will remain professional and selective. It must not use false scarcity, false deadlines, or unsupported guarantees.

## 6. Calls to Action and Lead Flow

Primary conversion channels:

- WhatsApp
- Direct message on the active platform

Primary keywords:

- `BRAND`
- `SOCIAL`
- `WEBSITE`

Example CTA: "Send `WEBSITE` by DM or WhatsApp for an initial review."

The exact WhatsApp URL or phone number must be supplied and verified before it is added to public captions or artwork. Until then, cards will refer generically to WhatsApp and DM without inventing contact details.

Organic publishing alone is not treated as a complete acquisition system. The operating rhythm also requires:

- Regular, relevant engagement with MENA business and founder accounts
- Prompt replies to incoming comments and messages
- A consistent qualification and follow-up process
- Weekly review of which subjects generate qualified conversations

## 7. Trello Board Design

The existing platform-based lists will be replaced by a single production workflow. A content concept exists once and carries all four platform adaptations inside one card.

### Lists

1. **Start Here** — campaign strategy, tone, templates, metrics, and operating instructions.
2. **Planned** — scheduled content ordered by publish date.
3. **Design in Progress** — content actively being designed.
4. **Review** — artwork and copy awaiting internal approval or revision.
5. **Ready to Publish** — approved assets and captions.
6. **Published** — final posts with live links and performance notes.

The board will not use separate lists for social platforms because that duplicates concepts and hides production status.

### Labels

Labels will be limited to the three services to preserve visual clarity:

- Branding
- Social Media
- Websites

The month, week, and content type will be expressed in the card title and description rather than through additional label clutter.

### Naming convention

`M01 W01 P01 — Brand clarity before visual identity`

The identifier remains stable while the card moves through the workflow.

### Card structure

Every production card contains the following sections:

1. **Purpose** — the business problem, objective, and intended action.
2. **Format** — static, carousel, checklist, comparison, testimonial, case study, or before-and-after.
3. **On-design copy** — exact headline and exact text for every frame or slide.
4. **Visual direction** — layout, imagery, hierarchy, and asset guidance.
5. **LinkedIn caption**
6. **Instagram caption**
7. **Facebook caption**
8. **X caption**
9. **CTA** — service keyword and contact path.
10. **Required assets** — project images, logos, screenshots, testimonials, or references.
11. **Approval notes** — any factual or client-use checks that must be completed.

### Card checklist

- Facts and claims verified
- Required assets collected
- Master design completed
- LinkedIn adaptation completed
- Instagram adaptation completed
- Facebook adaptation completed
- X adaptation completed
- Copy and artwork reviewed
- Final approval received
- Published to LinkedIn
- Published to Instagram
- Published to Facebook
- Published to X
- Live links and initial results recorded

## 8. Existing Board Content

The existing strategy card and nine platform-specific posts contain useful themes, but they are too narrow for the approved audience and do not support production tracking.

During implementation:

- Useful ideas will be rewritten for broad MENA SMB relevance.
- Duplicated platform cards will be consolidated into single multi-platform concept cards.
- Existing cards will not be deleted without an explicit recoverable migration step.
- The old lists will be archived only after their useful content has been transferred and verified.

## 9. Measurement

The primary measure is qualified demand, not follower growth alone.

Track weekly:

- Qualified direct messages
- Qualified WhatsApp enquiries
- Discovery calls booked
- Proposals requested or sent
- New customers influenced by social media
- Saves, shares, profile visits, and link clicks
- Best-performing topic, format, service, and CTA

The **Start Here** list will contain one results card with a simple weekly log. Performance insights will determine which content is refreshed during buffer days and which themes receive more weight in later months.

No customer-acquisition outcome is guaranteed. The plan is designed to improve the probability of qualified conversations through consistent publishing, credible proof, platform adaptation, and fast follow-up.

## 10. Quality Controls

Before a card reaches **Ready to Publish**, verify that:

- The design contains one clear idea and follows the Vellox visual system.
- The copy is specific to a recognizable MENA SMB problem.
- The tone is confident and professional, not playful or overly friendly.
- Each platform caption is properly adapted.
- The CTA matches the service discussed.
- Names, quotations, metrics, and results are factually approved.
- No unsupported guarantee, false scarcity, or invented proof is present.
- The artwork remains readable in every required platform size.
- The card includes all final assets and publishing information.

## 11. Implementation Boundary

The implementation phase will:

- Restructure the existing Trello board into the approved workflow.
- Preserve and migrate useful existing content.
- Create the Start Here reference cards.
- Create and fully write all 144 core content cards.
- Add labels, checklists, and publish scheduling in manageable batches.
- Verify card counts, required sections, platform captions, and workflow placement.

Public posting, client outreach, and sending messages are outside the board-build phase unless separately requested.
